# SECOND-BRAIN-KNOWLEDGE-PAPER.md — Global Crisis & Survival Preparedness Strategist

A curated knowledge base of research papers, books, and authoritative sources underpinning this skill's methodology. These should be consulted (and their principles extracted into skill reference files) when building the actual skill.

> **Note on sourcing:** This list was compiled from general subject-matter knowledge to give the skill a strong research foundation to build from. Before relying on any specific citation (e.g., in an academic, legal, or professional deliverable), verify the exact title, year, and venue independently, as this list has not been individually re-verified against live databases at compilation time.

## Research Papers & Sources (20)

1. FEMA (2021). Ready.gov: Build a Kit. U.S. Federal Emergency Management Agency — Foundational U.S. household emergency-preparedness framework.
2. World Health Organization (2019). Pandemic Influenza Risk Management: A WHO Guide. WHO — Foundational pandemic-preparedness planning guidance.
3. CDC (2021). Preparedness 101: Zombie Apocalypse (and companion All-Hazards Preparedness materials). Centers for Disease Control and Prevention — Widely cited public-health approach to popularizing all-hazards preparedness education.
4. WHO (2005). International Health Regulations (2005). World Health Organization — Foundational international framework for pandemic/public-health-emergency response coordination.
5. D. Auf der Heide (1989). Disaster Response: Principles of Preparation and Coordination. C.V. Mosby — Foundational disaster-response coordination methodology.
6. American Red Cross (2020). Family Disaster Plan and Preparedness Guide. American Red Cross — Applied household emergency-planning framework.
7. E. Auerbach (2016). Wilderness Medicine (7th ed.). Elsevier — Comprehensive evidence-based field-medicine/first-aid reference for delayed-care situations.
8. National Association of EMTs (2020). PHTLS: Prehospital Trauma Life Support (9th ed.). Jones & Bartlett — Standard first-aid/trauma-response training reference.
9. World Health Organization/War Trauma Foundation (2011). Psychological First Aid: Guide for Field Workers. WHO — Foundational Psychological First Aid framework for crisis coping.
10. B. Norris, S. Stevens, B. Pfefferbaum, K. Wyche, R. Pfefferbaum (2008). Community Resilience as a Metaphor, Theory, Set of Capacities, and Strategy for Disaster Readiness. American Journal of Community Psychology — Foundational community-resilience theoretical framework.
11. D. Aldrich (2012). Building Resilience: Social Capital in Post-Disaster Recovery. University of Chicago Press — Empirical research on mutual-aid/social-capital's role in crisis survival and recovery.
12. CDC (2020). Interim Infection Prevention and Control Recommendations. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention — Applied pandemic-hygiene and isolation-protocol guidance.
13. S. Cutter, L. Barnes, M. Berry, C. Burton, E. Evans, E. Tate, J. Webb (2008). A Place-Based Model for Understanding Community Resilience to Natural Disasters. Global Environmental Change — Foundational resilience-modeling framework.
14. World Food Programme (2019). Food Storage and Preservation Manual for Emergency Contexts. WFP — Applied food-storage/rotation methodology for prolonged-crisis planning.
15. WHO (2011). Technical Notes on Drinking-Water, Sanitation and Hygiene in Emergencies. World Health Organization — Standard water-purification and sanitation methodology for crisis contexts.
16. American Psychological Association (2020). Building Your Resilience. APA — Evidence-based psychological-resilience strategy reference.
17. National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health (NIOSH) (2018). Emergency Response Resources. CDC/NIOSH — Applied occupational/community emergency-response reference.
18. K. Tierney (2014). The Social Roots of Risk: Producing Disasters, Promoting Resilience. Stanford University Press — Sociological framework for understanding and building disaster resilience.
19. J. Auerbach, E. Miller (2020). COVID-19 Exposes the Cracks in Our Already Fragile Mental Health System. American Journal of Public Health — Evidence on pandemic-specific psychological-preparedness needs.
20. UNDRR (2015). Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction 2015-2030. United Nations Office for Disaster Risk Reduction — Global framework contextualizing individual/community preparedness within broader DRR strategy.
